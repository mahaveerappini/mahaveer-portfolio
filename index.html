About Me

I'm a Data Engineer focused on building scalable, reliable and production-ready data systems.

Data Engineer with 4+ years of experience designing and scaling batch and real-time streaming pipelines across healthcare and banking. I build systems that move fast and stay reliable, cutting data latency by up to 96%, processing 2TB+ of daily data, and maintaining 99%+ pipeline success rates in production.

My core stack is Python, SQL, PySpark, Apache Kafka, Airflow, and dbt, with deep experience across AWS (S3, Glue, EMR, Redshift, Athena, DMS) and GCP, plus Snowflake and BigQuery for warehousing.

That combination means I don't just build pipelines that work — I build ones that hold up to audit, scale under real transaction volume, and stay maintainable as requirements shift.
